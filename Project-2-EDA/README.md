# Project 2 - Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is part of my Data Analytics Internship at DecodeLabs.

After completing the Data Cleaning project, I performed an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the cleaned dataset.

The main objective of this project was to better understand the data, identify patterns, analyze relationships between variables, and extract useful insights.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The analysis was performed on the cleaned dataset produced in Project 1.

- Number of rows: **1,200**
- Number of columns: **14**
- Period covered: **January 2023 to June 2025**

The dataset contains information about orders, products, customers, prices, payment methods, order status, coupons, referral sources, and total revenue.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project were:

- Understand the structure of the dataset
- Calculate descriptive statistics
- Analyze the distribution of numerical and categorical variables
- Study correlations between numerical variables
- Analyze sales and order trends over time
- Detect potential outliers
- Identify important business insights
- Create visualizations to better understand the data

---

## 📈 Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Understanding

First, I explored the dataset to understand its structure, columns, data types, and time period.

The dataset contains **1,200 records and 14 columns**.

The numerical variables analyzed were:

- Quantity
- UnitPrice
- TotalPrice
- ItemsInCart

The categorical variables included:

- Product
- PaymentMethod
- OrderStatus
- CouponCode
- ReferralSource

---

### 2. Descriptive Statistics

I calculated basic statistics such as:

- Count
- Mean
- Median
- Standard deviation
- Minimum
- Maximum

Some results:

- Average quantity per order: **2.95**
- Average unit price: **356.41**
- Average order value: **1053.97**
- Average number of items in cart: **5.48**

---

### 3. Product Analysis

I analyzed the number of orders and total revenue generated by each product.

The product generating the highest revenue was:

🥇 **Chair**

It generated approximately **15.5% of the total revenue**.

---

### 4. Payment Method Analysis

Different payment methods were compared based on the average order value.

The highest average order value was associated with:

💳 **Credit Card**

---

### 5. Correlation Analysis

A correlation matrix and heatmap were created to analyze relationships between numerical variables.

The strongest correlation was found between:

**UnitPrice and TotalPrice**

Correlation coefficient:

**r = 0.72**

This relationship is expected because the total price is directly influenced by the unit price.

---

### 6. Trend Analysis

Monthly and yearly trends were analyzed using:

- Total revenue
- Number of orders
- Average order value

The best month in terms of revenue was:

📈 **June 2024**

The lowest month was:

📉 **April 2023**

To make a fair comparison, I also compared the first six months of each year.

From 2023 to 2025, the revenue for the January–June period decreased by approximately:

**19.1%**

---

### 7. Order Status Analysis

The distribution of order statuses was also analyzed.

The results showed:

- Cancelled orders: **20.8%**
- Returned orders: **20.6%**

These rates could be useful for further investigation to understand why a relatively high number of orders are cancelled or returned.

---

### 8. Coupon Analysis

I also analyzed coupon usage.

The results showed that:

🎟️ **74.2% of orders used a coupon**

This indicates that discounts and promotions may play an important role in customer purchasing behavior.

---

### 9. Outlier Detection

The IQR (Interquartile Range) method was used to detect potential outliers.

Results:

- Quantity: **0 outliers**
- UnitPrice: **0 outliers**
- TotalPrice: **8 outliers**
- ItemsInCart: **0 outliers**

The outliers detected in TotalPrice may represent large orders rather than data errors, so they should be investigated before being removed.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

Some of the main insights from this analysis are:

- **Chair** generated the highest total revenue.
- **Credit Card** had the highest average order value.
- **UnitPrice and TotalPrice** showed the strongest correlation.
- Revenue changed over time, with the best performance in **June 2024**.
- Revenue for the first half of the year decreased by approximately **19.1% between 2023 and 2025**.
- Around **20% of orders were cancelled** and around **20% were returned**.
- **74.2% of orders used a coupon**.
- Only **8 potential outliers** were detected in TotalPrice.
